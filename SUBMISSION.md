# Qorbitpay — Competition Submission Description (draft)

Qorbitpay brings the stock market into Nimiq Pay. Open the Mini App and your wallet already holds a brokerage-style portfolio: 1,278 tokenized US stocks and ETFs from Ondo and xStocks, including NVIDIA, Tesla, Apple and the S&P 500, all live on BNB Chain.

There's no sign-up and no backend. Your wallet is your identity, and every balance is read straight from the chain. The Market lists every token with live prices, and Asset Detail shows recent price action and each issuer's terms.

Buying is fast. Swaps route through the KyberSwap aggregator for the best price across BNB Chain liquidity, and the review screen shows every cost as its own line: quote, 0.5% platform fee, minimum received, price impact and network fee. Qorbitpay never holds your funds. Before you sign, the app decodes the transaction and checks the recipient, tokens, amount, fee and slippage floor against what you reviewed. You approve the exact amount, then swap, each step confirmed in Nimiq Pay's dialog.

Gifting is where Nimiq shines. Send any fraction of a share to a friend and add an optional tip in USDT, or in NIM through the Nimiq Mini App SDK. A share of Apple with a NIM tip on top, delivered in seconds.

The design follows a "Ledger" direction: ivory paper, forest-green ink, gold rules and an italic serif wordmark. It feels like a stock certificate you can trust with real holdings, not another dark-mode trading dashboard.

Open source (MIT). Tokenized stocks are not available to US persons.
